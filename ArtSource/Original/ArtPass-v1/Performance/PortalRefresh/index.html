Rail Arena / bounded distant portal refresh

Sky Gardens reuses very small distant destination images for two or four rendered frames, with an age limit of 1/15 second. Nearby (within 12 m), large and scoped apertures remain full rate. Projection/layer changes, re-entry, resize and substantial movement/turning force a refresh. Actual cached crop coordinates stay paired with the texture. Older-map defaults and all traversal/gameplay authority are preserved.
