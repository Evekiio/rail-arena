SKY GARDENS / DETAIL BLUEPRINT v2

STATUS: MEASURED ASSET CANDIDATES FOR REVIEW. NOT A PLAYABLE MAP.
V1 and all existing gameplay maps/builds are preserved.

WHAT IS NEW
62 measured sheets: 6 overall orthographic views, 5 layers, 3 sections and
48 detail views (6 per family). SVG vector originals and PNG previews match.
Eight asset families: StoneKit, Arch, SummitCanopy, PortalKit, Tree, Garden,
Ivy and Waterfall. See AssetRegister.json for exact component IDs and bounds.
The geometry is derived from Scene.json with deterministic seed 73029.

GEOMETRY CONTRACT
Metres throughout. Blender/canonical: X east, Y north, Z up.
Unity coordinates: (canonical X, canonical Z, -canonical Y).
All original collision objects retain their exact V1 specification.
New art is non-colliding. Cover caps rise 10 mm above their original proxies;
other insets stay within the proxy. These details require intersection review
when integrated with gameplay. Portal jambs/header/back are modeled art;
their gameplay collision is still a separate future integration task.
Four pad trajectories, summit platform placement and all base routes are unchanged.

DETAIL DECISIONS
Summit canopy: inner radius 6.1 m, outer 6.7 m; h=27.00 to 27.36 m.
Each quadrant has a 14-degree central break and two measured arc fragments.
Columns retain their original positions and heights, with 0.92 m capitals.
Underpass arches: 6 m clear span, 2 m spring height, 3.2 m inner crown,
3.38 m outer crown, 0.24 m extrusion depth; two arch skins on existing piers.
Trees have tapered segmented trunks and branches plus closed, cambered leaf blades.
Leaf blade thickness is 4 mm. Tree, shrubs, grass and ivy use seeded placements.
Waterfall rest surfaces descend 20 m: 64 vertical by 8 horizontal quads,
with three narrow foam ribbons, a bronze lip and defined static ripples.
Water and foam use double-sided review materials in the Unity importer.

FILES / REPRODUCE
Scene.json: canonical geometry specification including all explicit detail vertices.
MeshData.json: triangulated, local-origin meshes for direct Unity reproduction.
SkyGardens-Construction.blend: named, grouped editable detailed master.
SkyGardens-Construction.fbx / .glb: portable exports. GLB is round-trip verified.
Detailed-Overview/Garden/Summit.png are actual Blender review renders of this mesh
revision under neutral lighting, not in-game screenshots or AI-generated concepts.
Reproduce manifest: python tools/build_sky_detail_manifest.py
Reproduce sheets: python tools/build_sky_detail_sheets.py
Reproduce models: background Blender executes tools/build_sky_detail_construction.py
All scripts live at the repository root under tools/. Run headlessly.

UNITY
Copy the CONTENTS of UnityReadyAssets into a Unity project's Assets folder,
keeping all GUID .meta files. Open Assets/Validation/SkyGardensDetailV2Final/
SkyGardensConstruction.unity. This construction scene has review materials and
original collision proxies. It intentionally has no ArenaSession/player setup.
Alternatively place UnityDetailBuilder.cs in an Editor folder and run its Build
method headlessly with --sky-blueprint-root pointing at this package.
Its output folder must not already exist. Build settings are not modified.
The direct mesh-data route was verified; native FBX import has not been qualified.

VALIDATION / LIMITS
Blender-Validation.json and Unity-Validation.json document geometry bounds checks.
Preservation.json verifies V1 collision objects and the saved V1 package.
These checks do not establish production readiness, exact AI-image reproduction,
controller traversal, multiplayer behavior, combat balance, sightline acceptance,
LOD/performance qualification, UV/texturing quality or motion/audio comfort.
Review palette is stored in Blender materials and UnityDetailBuilder.cs; PBR
renderers may differ visually. Detailed UV atlases, stone wear, lighting/cloud
integration, foliage wind and waterfall/foam animation remain production work.
Summit dimensions and all new decorative forms remain pending creator approval.
