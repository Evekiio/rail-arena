## Requested direction

- Broad upper beam and substantial cheek rest.
- Thick angled shoulder pad at the rear.
- Reinforced lower brace with one compact opening.
- Small recessed sling socket; remove the old cyan circular stock disc.
- Adapt the form to ivory ceramic, graphite contact surfaces, teal and restrained cyan accents.

The supplied photo has the shoulder pad on its right and attachment on its left. On the right-facing Rail Arena rifle, the shoulder pad belongs on the left and the receiver interface on the right. This is a functional orientation change, not an instruction to reverse the rest of the rifle.

The receiver, barrel, optic, grip and overall weapon direction remain the existing design. This proposal explores the stock silhouette and visual integration. It does not establish exact dimensions, a new hand calibration, material acceptance or final geometry.
