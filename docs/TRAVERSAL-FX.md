# Twin Platforms traversal effects

Portals use animated opaque vortex fields, counter-rotating energy arcs, an expanding crossing ripple and short radial streaks. Both ends play distinct spatial entry/arrival sounds. Jump pads use concentric moving charge rings, a proximity charge field, visual coil compression/rebound and a launch burst with an original synthesized whoosh.

All sounds are original procedural mono synthesis. Quiet seamless idle hums fade spatially over 18 metres; event sounds attenuate over 42 metres. Master and Effects settings apply. Reduced Flash dims/slows the fields, reduces ripple intensity and removes the short streaks. Offline pause freezes animation and pauses audio. These effects add no camera shake.

Presentation events are keyed by actor teleport serial / pad ready tick. Prediction replay produces no events; matching authoritative snapshots cannot repeat a predicted cue. Historical round/life snapshots seed event counters silently. Simultaneous activations within 180 ms coalesce to avoid stacking sounds.

Physics, reciprocal portal routing, collision surfaces, jump destinations, launch velocity, movement, rifle tuning and network protocol remain unchanged. Only the collider-free pad coil animates; portal face and traversal roots never move.

Build: `Builds/TraversalFX-2026-10-29/RailArena.exe`. Keep the executable beside its data and runtime files. Test both blue/red portals and both pads, Effects mute, Reduced Flash and pause/resume. Automated checks do not establish sound-on balance, motion comfort or physical input acceptance.

The subsequent PortalViews build replaces the portal centre with a live destination image while retaining the edge energy effects. See [Portal views and floating health](PORTAL-VIEWS-AND-HEALTH.md).
