## Priorities and completion criteria

1. **Restore trustworthy current-build qualification.** Prevent tests from saving production scenes/assets; create their output directories explicitly. Bind test reports, source identity and a fresh build together. Make the runners' default bot count agree with the implemented three-bot cap, and separate graphics-dependent capture checks from no-graphics logic validation. Preserve historical reports.
2. **Complete the shared match/combat rules.** Deliver damage, time/score ending and regeneration as one bounded milestone. Test exact tick boundaries, damage combinations, sudden death, practice exemptions, death/respawn and rematch resets. Both duel peers must agree on health and final results.
3. **Qualify duel movement, roster agreement and safer spawns.** Investigate the recorded correction spikes and command clamps before extending traversal; capture distributions and causes rather than hiding them behind camera smoothing. Add versioned character negotiation and conflict handling; qualify spawn exposure. Preserve existing unique offline rosters and appearance-matched arms.
4. **Qualify the arena and portal system.** Hold movement tuning constant during comparisons. Prove controller clearance, bot traversal and network discontinuities before final environment detail. Keep the existing playable arena available for comparison.
5. **Finish presentation, settings and audio.** Work from specific in-motion/readability defects, complete approved settings and input flows, and review sound with the creator. Avoid rebuilding existing controls without evidence of a problem.
6. **Measure and prepare a review package.** Profile real four-contender rendering on recorded hardware, run full clean/impaired match-to-rematch regressions and package the qualified build. Human input, motion/audio, final art and actual remote-network acceptance remain separate gates.

No new weapons, cloud services, progression, monetization or additional modes are needed for this pass. Actual remote-peer availability should not block local enhancements.
